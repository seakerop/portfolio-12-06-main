Vigía

New

Market alerts with historical context.

Lets you set alerts on a watchlist, for example when Bitcoin's RSI drops below 30. Before each rule is saved, it is tested against the asset's history: how many times it would have fired and how the price moved over the following 5, 10 and 30 days compared with the average. That summary is shown with every alert.

  • Backtest over the full history using the same logic as the live alerts
  • Binomial test on independent cases and a separate analysis of the last three years
  • For Bitcoin, the current phase of the halving cycle with data from previous cycles

Thermometer

Data analysis

Do investment indicators anticipate future returns?

A study of 26 technical, sentiment, valuation and macroeconomic indicators against almost a century of S&P 500 data, and of 28 against the history of Bitcoin, at horizons from one day to ten years. Each data point is used only from its publication date, and the zones of each indicator are computed without later information.

  • Block bootstrap and Benjamini-Hochberg correction for multiple comparisons
  • Validation on independent periods and control for the time trend
  • Interactive reports in English and Spanish generated from the results

CineMatch

Leisure

An app for deciding what to watch as a couple or a group: everyone rates the same titles and the app shows the matches. It includes a shared list that keeps filling up and stores what is left to watch, real-time sessions across several phones and a single-phone mode taking turns. The catalogue comes from TMDB and each title is scored with its IMDb rating, weighted by number of votes, and with the awards recorded in Wikidata. You can ask for a balanced selection, award winners only, lesser-known gems or new releases. There is also a mode for choosing dinner, with the same game over types of food and a link to order on Uber Eats.
